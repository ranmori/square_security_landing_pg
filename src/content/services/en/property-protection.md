## Your business in safe hands

Theft, vandalism or unnoticed damage can seriously disrupt your operations. With facility and property protection, our security staff are right on site: they control access, patrol the premises regularly and react immediately when something is wrong.

This way damage is detected early and your operations continue undisturbed. Together with you, we define which tasks our staff take on and when they are on duty.

## Tailored to your property

Every building and every site is different. That is why we look at your property in advance and recommend measures that really fit – and adjust them when your requirements change.

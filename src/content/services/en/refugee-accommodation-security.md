## Security with respect and understanding

In asylum and refugee accommodation, people from many countries live together in a small space – often in a stressful situation. Security here needs more than presence: it needs empathy, language skills and experience.

Our security officers are multilingual and psychologically trained. They recognise tension early and resolve conflicts before they escalate. This creates a safe environment for residents, staff and the neighbourhood.

## Experience since 2020

We have been working in accommodation facilities since 2020 and have gained experience in a range of facilities. We work closely with facility management, social services and the responsible authorities.

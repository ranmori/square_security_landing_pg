## Security your event can rely on

Wherever many people come together, good preparation decides whether everything runs smoothly. We support you from the start: we advise you, create a security concept for your event and put it into practice with trained staff.

Our security officers ensure orderly entry, keep an eye on what is happening and step in calmly and firmly when needed. De-escalation always comes first – so your guests feel safe and welcome.

## Hands-on experience

Our assignments include the **Oktoberfest 2023** and the **Bunnycow Day Festival** on 15 June 2024. We bring this experience to every new event – whether it is a small company party or a large festival.

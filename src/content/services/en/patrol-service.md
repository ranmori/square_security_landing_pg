## Flexible security on the move

Not every property needs permanent guarding. With our patrol and district service, we check your buildings and grounds regularly – at fixed or deliberately varying times, so intruders cannot spot a pattern.

The patrol service works as a standalone service or as an addition to facility and property protection. It is ideal for commercial properties, public institutions, residential complexes and large sites.

## Clearly documented

Every check is logged. We report anything unusual to you immediately, so you can act quickly.

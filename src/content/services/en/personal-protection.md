## Protection that adapts to your life

For us, personal protection means security without it taking over your daily life. Our security officers discreetly accompany you to appointments, events or on your travels while keeping an eye on your surroundings.

## Individually planned

Before every assignment we hold a personal meeting, assess the threat level and plan routes and schedules together with you. The result is a protection concept that fits your situation.
